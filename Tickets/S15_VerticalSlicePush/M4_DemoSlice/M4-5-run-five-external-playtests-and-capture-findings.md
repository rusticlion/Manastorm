# M4-5: Run Five External Playtests And Capture Findings

**Goal:** Replace internal intuition with outside signal before expanding scope again.

## Tasks

### 1. Prepare A Playtest Build
- Use the locked demo roster and onboarding flow from the earlier M4 tickets.
- Make sure obvious debug noise and unfinished content are out of the way.

### 2. Define The Questions
- Ask each playtester questions like:
  - Did you understand the shared mana economy?
  - Could you tell why a spell was blocked or fizzled?
  - Which spells or characters felt unfair, weak, or confusing?
  - Did you feel outplayed or just lost in the information load?

### 3. Capture Structured Notes
- Create a short findings doc under `docs/` or another agreed location.
- Organize findings by:
  - comprehension issues
  - balance concerns
  - UI/readability concerns
  - delight/high-interest moments

### 4. Convert Findings Into Follow-Up Work
- Turn repeated or high-severity findings into backlog tickets rather than leaving them as loose notes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- At least five external playtests are completed.
- Findings are captured in a structured written form, not memory.
- The next balancing/polish pass is informed by real player behavior.

## Dependencies

- This is the final ticket in the S15 sequence and depends on the rest of the demo slice being presentable.
